Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Purpose

This OAuth application is a private integration that connects the owner's Google Calendar to the owner's self-hosted Home Assistant instance. It is not a public service.

Data accessed

The application requests access to Google Calendar data (calendars and events) only to display events and trigger home automations within the owner's Home Assistant.

Storage and sharing

  • Data is processed and stored only on the owner's local Home Assistant server.
  • No data is sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party.
  • No data is used for advertising, analytics or profiling.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
